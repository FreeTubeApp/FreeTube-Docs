## Exporting a playlist

You can export all your FreeTube playlists in a single file or just export one of them.

- To export all your playlists go to `Settings->Data->Playlist->Export Playlist`
- To export an individual playlist go to the desired playlist and click on the "Export This Playlist" button. Make sure to select "Export FreeTube (.db)" if you wish to import it to another instance of FreeTube.

## Importing a playlist

To import a FreeTube playlist from a .db file go to `Settings->Data->Playlist->Import Playlist`
